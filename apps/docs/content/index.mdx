# Tikket docs

## What Tikket is

Tikket is open-source scheduling for teams, licensed under MIT. It adds team scheduling to the community edition of Cal.com:

- Team management and assignment
- Shared team event types
- Roles and invites
- Round-robin with weights and priorities
- Collective events

You can run Tikket on your own server from the source repository at https://github.com/threos/tikket.dev. You can also use the hosted version at https://tikket.dev. The features of the hosted version (billing and plan limits) are optional, and a self-hosted Tikket runs without them.

## Tikket, Cal.diy and Cal.com

Cal.com is the commercial scheduling product of Cal.com, Inc. Cal.diy is the MIT community edition of Cal.com, with the enterprise features removed: teams, organizations, insights, workflows, routing forms and SSO. Tikket is a fork of Cal.diy that adds team scheduling back under the MIT license. Routing forms, workflows, organizations, insights, SSO and SAML are not in Tikket. Package names such as `@calcom/*` and the `calendso` database name come from the upstream projects, and Tikket keeps them.
