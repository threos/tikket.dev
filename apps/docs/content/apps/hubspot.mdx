# HubSpot

With the HubSpot app, Tikket creates a meeting in HubSpot for each booking with one of your HubSpot contacts. The app needs a client ID and a client secret from a HubSpot developer account.

## Get the HubSpot client ID and secret

1. Go to [HubSpot Developer](https://developers.hubspot.com/) and sign in to your account, or create a new one.
2. From the developer account home page, go to "Manage apps".
3. Click "Create app" at the top right of the page.
4. In the "App info" tab, enter the information that you want for your app.
5. Open the "Auth" tab.
6. Copy the client ID and the client secret into your `.env` file under these fields:

    ```
    HUBSPOT_CLIENT_ID
    HUBSPOT_CLIENT_SECRET
    ```

7. Set the redirect URL for OAuth to this URL. Replace `<Tikket URL>` with the URL where your Tikket instance runs.

    ```
    <Tikket URL>/api/integrations/hubspot/callback
    ```

8. In the "Scopes" section, select "Read" and "Write" for the scope `crm.objects.contacts`.
9. Click "Save" at the bottom of the page.

Tikket now creates a meeting in HubSpot for each booking with your contacts.
